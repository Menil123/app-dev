# My Favorite Series
## The Midnight Signal
**Genre:** Science Fiction, Mystery, Thriller
**Description:** The Midnight Signal is a fictional series about five college students who discover a mysterious radio frequency that sends messages from the future. As they try to prevent disasters, they uncover secrets about their town and learn that changing the future can have dangerous consequences.
**Main Characters:**
- Ethan – The leader of the group
- Mia – A technology expert
- Lucas – A curious investigator
- Sarah – A brave and intelligent student
- Noah – The person who first discovers the signal
**Why I Like This Series:**
I like this series because it combines technology, mystery, and adventure. The story shows the importance of teamwork, friendship, and making responsible decisions.
